01 // Engineering Philosophy

Bridging community gaps with engineered systems

Why I write code and how I approach real-world software architecture.

I believe software development in developing economies should prioritize utility over vanity. As a Computer Science student at Cebu Institute of Technology – University, I have focused my development time on addressing everyday frictions around me: prescription transparency, informal small-business credit management, and urban commuter mobility.

Rather than building cookie-cutter clones of popular apps, my proudest engineering project to date is MiTinda—an offline-first PWA created specifically for my mother’s neighborhood karenderya (eatery). It solved real cashflow and inventory leakages through intuitive touch-first UX and localized ledger mechanisms.

My core technical foundation spans modern reactive frontend frameworks (React, Next.js), typed mobile development (React Native/Expo), structured relational storage (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite), and practical AI systems (Gemini API, RAG search with pgvector, and local OCR engines).

How I Build

  • 01
    Context First: Software must function under real constraints—intermittent connectivity, uncalibrated devices, and non-technical end users.
  • 02
    Honest Architecture: I don’t sprinkle microservices or complex architectures where a cleanly typed monolith or SQLite database solves the actual problem.
  • 03
    Ownership: From schema migration to UI micro-interactions and end-user testing, I take direct responsibility for every layer I ship.
